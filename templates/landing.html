Analytical Pipeline

Three distinct layers of diagnostic reasoning

Unlike uncalibrated black-box classifiers, our diagnostic pipeline decomposes pathology into verified anatomical evidence.

Diagnostic Phases
  1. 01 Neural Feature Extraction
  2. 02 Color-Space Tissue Quantification
  3. 03 Expert Rule Forward-Chaining

01. Deep Spectral Feature Extraction & Attribution

The specimen is processed through a convolutional transfer learning backbone (MobileNetV2 / ResNet50). Before presenting a verdict, Grad-CAM calculates the gradient of the top-predicted class score with respect to the final convolutional feature maps, guaranteeing that the model attended to foliar lesions rather than background soil or artifacts.

Convolutional Core
Stage A: Raw Tensor 224×224 RGB input tensor normalized by channel-wise mean and variance.
Stage B: Feature Maps High-level spatial activation in terminal convolutional bottleneck.
Stage C: Visual Attribution Rectified activation heatmap overlay highlighting necrotic tissue margins.

02. HSV Color-Space Tissue Quantification

Biometric Vision

Diagnostic decisions require understanding the severity of tissue destruction. The system segments healthy photosynthetic tissue from necrotic, chlorotic, and fungal lesion zones using mathematical color-space thresholding to output an exact percentage of leaf damage.

03. Forward-Chaining Agronomic Risk Engine

AI classification is combined with micro-climate variables (relative humidity, ambient temperature, precipitation volume, and crop growth stage). Prioritized inference rules determine spread probability and prescribe precise organic and synthetic chemical regimens.
